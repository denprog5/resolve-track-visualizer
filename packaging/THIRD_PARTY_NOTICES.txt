FFmpeg 9.0.1 and FFprobe
------------------------
The bundled programs are built from the unmodified official FFmpeg 9.0.1
source with GPL and version-3 terms enabled. This configured build is
GPL-3.0-or-later, compatible with the project's chosen license. Nonfree
components and external libraries are not enabled. The build starts from
--disable-everything and enables only the codecs, formats, filters, parsers,
and local protocols used for supported audio analysis, overlay rendering,
and probing. Network support, shared FFmpeg libraries, and autodetected
optional libraries are disabled. FFmpeg libraries are linked statically into
the programs; operating-system runtime libraries remain platform dependencies.

Official source URL:
https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-9.0.1.tar.xz
